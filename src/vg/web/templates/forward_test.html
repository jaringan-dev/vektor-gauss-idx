Corrected on 7 Nov 2026. Returns are now measured as a holder could have earned them: bought at the average price of the day after the signal (the signal only exists after the close), and adjusted for splits, bonus shares, rights issues and cash dividends using the exchange's own reference prices. The old method bought at the signal-day close and adjusted for splits only, which turned some rights issues into losses of up to 96%; its 5-day edge is kept in the "old method" column for comparison.
