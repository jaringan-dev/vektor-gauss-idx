How the test works, and its limits

  • Entry is the signal day's close; each later day is replayed in order. A gap through the stop exits at the open (worse than planned). If the stop and the target both fall inside one day's range, the stop is assumed to hit first.
  • Only signals with {{ horizon }} later trading days are used, so nothing is judged on partial information. Prices are adjusted for splits and bonus issues; cash dividends are not.
  • Signals from the newer broker patterns (BROKER2) only exist since June 2026 and were computed backwards. Several patterns have few independent observations because signals on the same day are related.
  • This does not model auto-rejection limits, order-queue priority, or slippage beyond the single cost assumption.
