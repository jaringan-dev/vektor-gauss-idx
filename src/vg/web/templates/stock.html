Who is buying and selling? {{ term('group_flow', 'Net flow by group') }} per day

The same {{ term('certain_bounds', 'certain range') }} as above, split into three groups that always add up to zero (every rupiah bought is sold by someone): foreign houses (the foreign institutional brokers), domestic retail brokers (the mass-retail apps and brokers) and all other brokers (everyone else, which mixes local institutions, bank-backed houses and smaller brokers). The groups come from the broker's code, not from who its client is, so "retail" and "institution" are an estimate. The three top panels share one scale, so a flat panel means a small share of the day's value, not missing data. The bottom panel shows at least how much of the day's value the same brokers both bought and sold ({{ term('roundtrip', 'round-trip') }}). That includes a broker's own clients trading against each other, so a high value is not proof of a market maker.
