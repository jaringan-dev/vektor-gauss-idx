Who is buying and selling? {{ term('group_flow', 'Net flow by group') }} per day

The same bars as above, split into three groups of brokers. Every rupiah bought is sold by someone, so the three groups always cancel out. Foreign houses are the foreign institutional brokers, domestic retail brokers are the mass-retail apps and brokers, and all other brokers are everyone else (local institutions, bank-backed houses and smaller brokers). The groups come from the broker's code, not from who its client is, so "retail" and "institution" are an estimate. The three top panels share one scale, so a flat panel means a small share of the day's value, not missing data. The bottom panel is different: it shows at least how much of the day's value the same brokers both bought and sold ({{ term('roundtrip', 'round-trip') }}). A broker's own clients trading against each other count too, so a high value is not proof of a market maker.
