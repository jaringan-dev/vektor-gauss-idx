What each column does

  • Raw edge: pattern 5-day return minus the average of all stocks that day (the number on the Track record page).
  • Matched edge: minus the average of stocks in the same size and liquidity bucket instead (25 buckets). If a pattern's raw edge shrinks here, it was mostly a small-stock effect.
  • After costs: the matched edge minus a cost estimated per signal (commission {{ '%.1f' % (params.fee * 100) }}%, the tick-size spread, and market impact for an order of Rp{{ '{:,}'.format(params.qty_idr) }}).
  • Intervals come from resampling blocks of {{ params.block }} days {{ '{:,}'.format(params.reps) }} times, which respects that signals on the same or neighbouring days are related.
  • q: corrects for testing many patterns at once. Folds +: share of half-year periods with a positive after-cost edge (the last {{ params.embargo }} days of each period are dropped).
  • Verdict: “Candidate” needs an after-cost interval above zero, q below 0.10, and a positive edge in at least 70% of the periods. Anything less is labelled for what it is.

Not removed: beta and sector effects (sectors need a map we do not have yet), and the real-world frictions the cost model cannot see.
